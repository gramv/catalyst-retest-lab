Measurement & audit

Evidence stays separate

US results use broker fills. Manual reports, engineering tests and Jev-managed trading do not enter the frozen V1 baseline. No combined market return is shown.

Paper-fill limitations

Impact, latency slippage and queue position are not modeled. Adjusted P&L is unavailable. Paper results do not establish achievable returns.

Measurements

Closed trades determine P&L and win rate, with breakevens counted. MFE/MAE use in-position observations. IEX is not consolidated; missing data stays unavailable.

Permanent history

Events are append-only and hash-chained. Corrections add records. Each result keeps its strategy version; today’s picks remain private until the official close.
