## Why the Friday geometry audit is retained but not routed

A fixed descriptive audit used all 78 regular-session five-minute bars, strict two-neighbor pivots, the latest intact pivot low as T, the latest earlier lower pivot low as S, and the nearest recorded pivot high above T as P. The research assumption M=T+15 bps was rounded down to cents. The cap convention is not a new application requirement. No farther target was selected to rescue 2R.

Across 25 stocks and two benchmarks,11 cases had resistance at/below M,9 had less than 2R, and 7 lacked a complete structure. **This narrow diagnostic is not an exhaustive search or a validated strategy.** It conservatively retains earlier intraday highs even if later crossed; those may no longer be meaningful resistance. Its result cannot establish that no profitable setup existed, that Jev was correct earlier, or that Monday will offer none. It does show why mechanically copying the latest Friday micro-pivot into Monday is not a sound handoff.

## Data quality and reproducibility

Alpaca clock/calendar and SIP daily data for AAPL/NVDA were retrieved successfully. Additional stock-bar/snapshot requests repeatedly returned connector internal errors. Public Yahoo chart observations were used for the remaining descriptive analysis; AAPL/NVDA last closes agree with the retrieved Alpaca data to cents. This cross-check is limited to those closes, not full vendor equivalence.

All 57 daily series contain 63 observations through September 18. The code validates OHLC ordering, unique dates, the last session and matching benchmark dates. Intraday files contain 79 rows; the 16:00row is excluded, leaving 78completed09:30–15:55 bars at 300 second intervals. Daily official close and final five-minute close differ for some names, and extrema differ slightly too. These series are kept separate; auction coverage, aggregation and vendor treatment are possible explanations, not established causes. The discrepancies must be reconciled against the authoritative feed before execution.

Calculations use Decimal arithmetic after rounding source float noise to 0.0001 USD. SMA20/50 use completed closes; ATR14 is a simple mean of true range, **not Wilder smoothing**. Relative performance is the 20-session price return minus SPY over matching dates, without dividends. Volume liquidity is 20-session close×volume, a research proxy; it is not the app's raw volume×provider VWAP admission calculation. The intraday typical-price volume average is also a proxy, not exact trade VWAP. Prices use the provider's OHLC field, not adjusted-close. Returned split/dividend metadata is retained, but corporate actions and provider adjustment semantics have not been independently reconciled. No current spread, fee schedule, borrow, asset eligibility or order-book claim is made.
