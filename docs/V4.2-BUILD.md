## Required implementation

- Muse remains the researcher. Jev evaluates supplied state; it does no browsing, source retrieval or
  prose generation. Risk remains the sole authorizer of every broker mutation.
- All model calls go through jev_review at the officially documented systemone endpoint with Bearer
  authentication. No direct TypeSafe calls elsewhere. Preserve exact request/response JSON bytes,
  timestamp, exact model ID and latency in immutable, verifiable receipts. A bare approval flag is invalid.
- Batch independent questions in one request. Every Choice question includes an Insufficient evidence
  option, including apparently binary questions. Score uses rubric levels; Noul is a 0–1 judgment.
- Build SKEPTIC before TRIAGE: stale news, priced-in evidence, unsupported inference, typed
  APPROVE/REJECT/NEEDS_REVIEW verdict. Rejection stops that thesis and records disagreement.
- TRIAGE judges up to 300 raw movers and produces at most 20 for Muse research, with receipts for all
  judgments. Freshness, extension and volume evidence are neutral inputs. Numeric thresholds and
  shortlist ordering/tie-breaks must be explicit coded policy; unresolved policy requires a ruling.
- TRACKING judgments are advisory. Log and refuse model-requested early exits until cancellation,
  partial-exit, late-fill and reversal recovery are separately built and their tests passed.
- ai_decisions records candidate, model, stage (TRIAGE/SKEPTIC/ROUTING/TRACKING), question, answer,
  probability, decision_confidence, input_hash, latency_ms and receipt_id. Decision confidence is not
  trade win probability; confidence calibration is evaluated across groups of judgments.
- Evidence bundles contain candidate, short thesis, catalyst, short price/news excerpts, S/M/P and
  coded trigger state. No full articles, owner names/PII/emails, broker secrets/account IDs or wallets.
- ARM_ON_TRIGGER is a conditional model intent, never a broker permission. Before use, code verifies
  printed trigger, spread at most 10 bps, quote age at most 5 seconds, open regular session, no daily
  halt, unexpired intent and valid risk reservation. Failure kills/logs the intent. Final transport still
  requires an exact-request, unexpired, one-use database risk decision.
- Event delivery uses persistent storage and heartbeats. A lapsed heartbeat means DOWN. Every event
  has idempotency; authorizations expire. Duplicate/expired intents are rejected and logged.
- 429/529 retry uses bounded exponential backoff and jitter. After a configured consecutive-failure
  threshold, open the circuit for a cooldown. Down/slow/uncertain means NEEDS_REVIEW, never implicit
  approval or silently skipped work. Log app-observed latency/error rates. A fallback, if later enabled,
  must use the same adapter, receipts and question contract.
- All numerics, dates, session/expiry comparisons, sizing, risk and R computations stay in code.
  Existing 1%/2%/-3% risk rules, durable halts, correlation limits and immutable ledger remain mandatory.
- Jev-active candidates/trades have immutable separate cohort attribution. Never merge their results
  into the Jev-free V1 baseline. Engineering tests remain a third excluded population.
- Do not claim unattended operation before secure credential calls, persistent workers, restart-safe
  queues/state and deadline-bounded decisions are actually demonstrated. Until then: supervised paper.

## Final frozen mechanics and precedence

Longs only; S < T <= M < P. A regular-session printed trade at or below T completes the retest.
Confirmation also requires current ask <= M, spread <= 10 bps, quote age <= 5 seconds, healthy data,
an open session and an unexpired entry window. Stop-before-trigger, excessive entry price and prolonged
feed failure invalidate. A quote/bar alone cannot create a touch. Entry is a whole-share DAY limit at M,
with fixed S/P bracket legs. No extra reclaim/breakout criterion is permitted.

Minimum reward is twice stop distance at executable entry M. Size is floor(1% equity/(M-S)), with
no-leverage cap; 2% total risk and -3% cancel-and-flatten halt remain frozen. Entry eligibility ends by
calendar flatten time (15:55 on a regular day), even though DAY/session expiry is official close.
The official early-close calendar rule remains in force. Final transport rechecks this deadline.
Old event records are not recomputed under the clarified admission rule. These rulings are recorded
with provenance in CONTRACT-RESOLUTIONS.md; no strategy version is silently introduced.
